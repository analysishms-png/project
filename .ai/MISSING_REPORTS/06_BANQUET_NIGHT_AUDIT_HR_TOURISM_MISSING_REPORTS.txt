REPORT 2: NightAuditReport
============================================================
HMS.bas FORM NAME: NightAuditReport
HMS.bas LINE: 40
BUSINESS PURPOSE: Night audit report showing daily reconciliation
  — room revenue, POS, payments, outstanding, occupancy.

HMS.bas SQL LOGIC:
  SELECT
    -- Occupancy
    (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM RoomOcc
     WHERE Type NOT IN ('C','O')
     AND PropertyID = @pid) AS OccupiedRooms,

    -- Revenue
    (SELECT ISNULL(SUM(AmtDr),0) FROM PayCharge
     WHERE Vdate = @date AND RoomType <> 'RO'
     AND PropertyID = @pid) AS FORoomRevenue,

    -- POS
    (SELECT ISNULL(SUM(BillAmt),0) FROM Bill
     WHERE Vdate = @date AND CancelYN <> 'Y'
     AND PropertyID = @pid) AS POSRevenue,

    -- Payments
    (SELECT ISNULL(SUM(AmtCr),0) FROM PayCharge
     WHERE Vdate = @date AND PropertyID = @pid) AS TotalPayments,

    -- Audit Trail
    SELECT * FROM NightAuditLog
    WHERE AuditDate = @date AND PropertyID = @pid

  - Date
  - Property

REPORT 4: OccAnalysis (Occupancy Analysis)
============================================================
HMS.bas FORM NAME: OccAnalysis
HMS.bas LINE: 661102
BUSINESS PURPOSE: Period-wise occupancy analysis with %
  occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue.

HMS.bas SQL LOGIC:
  SELECT
    @date AS AuditDate,
    (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM RoomMast WHERE Type='RO'
     AND LogSite_Code=@site) AS TotalRooms,
    (SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT RoomNo) FROM RoomOcc
     WHERE Type NOT IN ('C','O')
     AND PropertyID=@pid) AS OccupiedRooms,
    CAST((SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT RoomNo) FROM RoomOcc
     WHERE Type NOT IN ('C','O')
     AND PropertyID=@pid) AS FLOAT)
    / (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM RoomMast WHERE Type='RO'
     AND LogSite_Code=@site) * 100 AS OccPercent,
    (SELECT ISNULL(SUM(AmtDr),0) FROM PayCharge
     WHERE Vdate=@date AND RoomType<>'RO'
     AND PropertyID=@pid) AS Revenue

  - Date From / Date To
  - Room Type
