REPORT 4: LuxuryTaxRegister
============================================================
HMS.bas FORM NAME: LuxuryTaxRegister
HMS.bas LINE: 5118
BUSINESS PURPOSE: Luxury tax register showing all luxury tax
  collected on room rent.

HMS.bas SQL LOGIC:
  SELECT pc.Vdate, pc.RoomNo, pc.Vno,
    gf.Name AS GuestName,
    pc.AmtDr AS RoomCharge,
    pc.LuxTaxAmt AS LuxuryTax,
    pc.TaxCode1, pc.TaxAmt1,
    pc.TaxCode2, pc.TaxAmt2
  FROM PayCharge pc
  LEFT JOIN GuestFolio gf ON pc.FolioNoDocId = gf.DocId
  WHERE pc.Vdate BETWEEN @fromdate AND @todate
  AND pc.LuxTaxAmt > 0
  AND pc.PropertyID = @propertyid
  ORDER BY pc.Vdate, pc.RoomNo

HMS.bas LINE: 480000
BUSINESS PURPOSE: Detailed tax invoice showing each tax
  component on each charge.

HMS.bas SQL LOGIC:

    gf.Name AS GuestName,
    rm.Name AS RevenueName,
    pc.AmtDr AS TaxableAmount,

    pc.TaxCode2, pc.TaxAmt2,
    pc.TaxCode3, pc.TaxAmt3,
